# 🚀 DAILYCREW

### Verified People. Trusted Work.

DAILYCREW is a next-generation workforce marketplace designed to connect verified workers with verified businesses for daily, part-time, shift-based, temporary, and event-based jobs.

The platform helps businesses hire reliable workers quickly while helping workers find genuine job opportunities near their location.

---

# 📌 Problem Statement

Many businesses such as canteens, hotels, restaurants, catering services, and event organizers struggle to find reliable workers at short notice.

At the same time, thousands of workers struggle to find genuine daily jobs with transparent payments and trusted employers.

DAILYCREW solves this problem by creating a trusted workforce ecosystem powered by verification, location intelligence, ratings, and smart matching.

---

# 🎯 Vision

To become India's most trusted workforce network by connecting businesses and workers through a secure, transparent, and intelligent hiring platform.
